# Dashboard Specification

The gold datasets are designed for a fleet operations dashboard with four pages.

## Fleet Health

- Vehicles by warranty risk band
- Average latest SOH by model
- Vehicles with fastest observed SOH drop
- Equivalent full cycles by vehicle

## Charging Intelligence

- Charging sessions by German region
- DC fast-charging share by region
- Average energy added per session
- Average charging power by region

## Thermal Risk

- Elevated and critical thermal events over time
- Thermal risk by region
- High-temperature events during charging vs driving
- Vehicle drilldown for repeated thermal exposure

## Warranty Monitoring

- Vehicle-level warranty risk score
- SOH drop vs odometer
- DC fast-charging share vs thermal exposure
- Priority list for service or engineering review
